Future physician · Aging & neuroscience research

Ava George

I envision a future where older adults receive the support they need while preserving their independence and sense of self. By exploring the connections between medicine, engineering, and emerging technologies, I hope to build tools that make aging safer and help older adults keep doing what matters most to them.

An IB Diploma candidate at Centennial High School, I also study at Norco and Fullerton colleges and research machine learning for fall prediction, Alzheimer’s detection, and drug discovery. My work includes seven IEEE papers (five as first author), a first-author journal article, a patent-pending fall prediction system, and selection as a Regeneron ISEF finalist.

Beyond research, I shadow physicians, serve older adults through the Osher Lifelong Learning Institute, and mentor 114 high school researchers nationwide as Lab Manager of Jahanikia NeuroLab.
